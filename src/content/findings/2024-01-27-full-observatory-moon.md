January's full moon, popularly known as the "Full Wolf Moon," rises above Las Campanas Observatory in this striking landscape photograph. The twin 6.5-meter Magellan telescopes appear prominently in the foreground. This scene was captured using a telephoto lens from approximately 9 miles away in Chile's Atacama Desert, with the moon positioned roughly 240,000 miles distant.
