This panoramic view assembles high-resolution scans from original film frames documenting the Moon's Sea of Tranquility. Captured 55 years ago by Neil Armstrong from the Eagle Lunar Module window shortly after landing on July 20, 1969, the leftmost frame represents the first picture taken by a person on another world. The composition shows thruster nozzles in the foreground (south side) and the Eagle's shadow on the right (west). A shallow crater visible on the right measures approximately 12 meters across. These photographs were taken about ninety minutes after landing, before lunar surface exploration, to document the site in case an emergency departure became necessary.
