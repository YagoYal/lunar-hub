The image displays Earth's complete shadow as visualized through a compilation of lunar eclipses. Since ancient times, observers noted that Earth's dark shadow on the Moon during a partial lunar eclipse is circular. Modern digital techniques allowed an astrophotographer to combine 22 years of lunar eclipse imagery to reveal the full extent of our planet's shadow.

The Moon doesn't experience eclipses monthly because its orbital path is slightly inclined relative to Earth's orbit. Some lunar eclipse photographs reveal a faint blue band where Earth's atmosphere filters out more red sunlight than blue.
