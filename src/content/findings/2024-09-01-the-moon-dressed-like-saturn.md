The image shows Earth's foreground clouds passing in front of the Moon, creating an optical illusion that makes the lunar body resemble Saturn. The Moon displays a slight crescent phase with most of its surface visible by reflected Earthlight, in what astronomers call the Da Vinci glow. The bright lunar crescent receives direct sunlight from below the horizon, indicating the photo was taken before sunrise on December 24, 2019 — two days before a solar eclipse. In the background, lights from Guatemalan towns appear behind the volcano Pacaya.
