LUNARK is not a mission to the Moon — it's an Earth-based analog test of a Moon habitat design. SAGA Space Architects (Copenhagen/Hanoi), with more than 50 partners providing materials and equipment, built a lightweight, foldable structure combining origami-inspired paper-folding techniques with biomimicry. Architects Sebastian Aristotelis and Karl-Johan Sørensen deployed and lived in it in complete isolation in the Greenland Arctic to test its resilience and their own, under conditions meant to approximate parts of the lunar environment's difficulty.

`publishedDate` above uses the expedition's start month (September 2020); the mission ran through November 2020.
