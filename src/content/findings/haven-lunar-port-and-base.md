HAVEN is a 2020 diploma thesis (TU Wien) proposing an integrated lunar arrival port: a landing zone, storage hangar, and permanent habitat built partly from sustainably reused discarded lander components. It houses a core team of four astronauts plus rotating four-person crews undergoing ten-day acclimatization, balancing private quarters with shared spaces across a surface and a subsurface level connected by a "lunar staircase."
