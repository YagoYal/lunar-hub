If the full Moon suddenly faded, what would you see? The answer was recorded in a dramatic time-lapse video taken during the total lunar eclipse from Uruguay. During such an eclipse, Earth positions itself between the Moon and Sun, causing the Moon to fade dramatically. However, it never becomes completely dark because the Earth's atmosphere refracts some light onto it.

The video captures how the nighttime scene initially appears moonlit and bright. As the eclipse progresses and the Moon darkens, background stars become increasingly visible. The most striking moment occurs when the sky surrounding the eclipsed moon suddenly appears to be full of stars and highlighted by the busy plane of our Milky Way Galaxy. After approximately two hours, the Moon emerges from Earth's shadow, and its bright glow once again dominates the sky.
