Venus was performing as Earth's brilliant evening star in June 2026, appearing alongside the Moon and other celestial objects in western skies. On June 17, the bright planet rose after sunset near a young crescent Moon. From certain locations, observers witnessed a lunar occultation — the Moon passing directly in front of Venus. In southern British Columbia, Canada, this dramatic event occurred during daylight hours. The photograph captures the moment following Venus' hour-long disappearance, as the evening star emerged beyond the bright lunar limb, showcasing the rare celestial alignment against cloudy skies.
